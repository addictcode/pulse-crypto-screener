Telegram

Alerts reach your phone with context in one message. Bursts arrive as a digest. Thresholds, mute and watchlist are set by chatting to the bot.

How it works

  1. Binance streamsTickers, candles, mark prices, liquidations and order books over WebSockets.
  2. Pulse engineJava backend keeps the market in memory, rebuilds books, scores walls and runs the detectors every 2 seconds.
  3. Your screenOnly changed numbers travel to the browser, twice a second, compressed to a few kilobytes.
